Support

Memory Pieces FAQ and how to reach us

How do I start?
Tap "New Puzzle" on the home screen, pick a photo, frame it and choose the number of pieces. Drag a piece near its spot on the board and it clicks into place.
What are the difficulty levels?
Easy 9 pieces (3×3), Normal 16 (4×4), Hard 36 (6×6) and Expert 64 (8×8). You can replay the same photo at another level (memory › Play Again).
What are hints and the guide image, and how do they affect best times?
💡 A hint puts one piece in place. You get 3 free hints per puzzle; after that, watch a short ad for each extra one (unlimited with Remove Ads). 🖼️ The guide lays the photo on the board in a faint gray. A game where you used a hint or the guide even once doesn't count toward best times.
It says my puzzle drawer is full
You can keep 3 puzzles in progress in the free version, or 6 with Remove Ads. Finish one, or long-press a puzzle in the drawer and choose "Remove from Drawer".
Is my puzzle saved?
Yes. It saves every time you move a piece, so closing the app or a phone call won't lose anything. After 90 seconds without a touch the timer pauses, and it carries on when you touch again.
Is Remove Ads a subscription? How do I restore it?
It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It removes all ads and grows the puzzle drawer to 6 slots. On a new device or after reinstalling, tap Settings › Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account (iPhone/iPad) or Google account (Android).
I'd like a refund
The store handles payments, so refunds are requested from the store: on iPhone and iPad at reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android in the Google Play app › Payments & subscriptions › Budget & history.
Ad settings and privacy choices
On iPhone and iPad, turn off tracking in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Where consent applies (such as the EEA and UK), use the app's Settings › Privacy Choices. On Android, delete your advertising ID in Settings › Privacy › Ads.
How do I delete my photos and records?
Use "Delete" on a memory or "Remove from Drawer" in the drawer. Deleting the app removes all its data. See section 5 of the privacy policy.
Which devices does it run on?
iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later (iPhone XS / XR and newer), and Android 8.0 or later.
Can I turn off the music or sounds?
Music, sound effects and haptics each have their own switch in Settings. If another app is playing music, the background music doesn't start.
