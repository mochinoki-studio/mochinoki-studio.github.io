Memory Pieces Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy explains how the operator of Memory Pieces ("we") handles information in the Memory Pieces apps for iPhone, iPad and Android ("the app") and on this website. It follows the structure required by Korea's Personal Information Protection Act (Article 30).

This English version is provided for reference. The Korean version is authoritative; if the two differ, the Korean version prevails.

1. Why information is processed

We process information only for these purposes, and will take the steps Korean law requires (PIPA Article 18) before using it for anything else:

  • Making jigsaw puzzles from your photos, saving your progress and keeping finished puzzles in your memory album
  • Showing ads in the free version (through Google AdMob) and asking for ad consent where the law requires it
  • Checking whether you own "Remove Ads"
  • Answering you if you contact us (for example by email)

We don't use information for anything else. We don't sell it, build profiles of you or combine it with other data.

2. What is processed

① Processed only on your device

The puzzle features work entirely on your device. The following is stored only in the app's own storage, is never sent to us, and disappears when you delete it or the app:

  • Photos: the cropped copy of each photo you pick, and a small thumbnail. Only the date the photo was taken is shown with your memory; location data in photos is not used.
  • Puzzle progress: piece positions, play time, hints used, whether the guide image was used
  • Memories: finished puzzles, the date finished, your time, the number of pieces and the note you write
  • Best times and settings: best time per difficulty; music, sound, haptics and board theme
  • Ad state: how many full-screen ads were shown and when (to limit how often), and your ad consent choice
  • Purchase state: whether you own "Remove Ads" (so the app can start without ads offline)

The app uses the system photo picker and reads only the photos you choose, one at a time. It does not ask for access to your whole photo library, camera, microphone, contacts or location.

② Sent off the device (ads, free version)

The free version includes Google's ad SDKs (Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform). When an ad is requested, these SDKs send the following directly to Google. We do not receive it.

DataAdvertising identifier (on iPhone, the IDFA only if you allow tracking; on Android, the advertising ID and app set ID), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device, OS and app information (model, OS version, language, app version), ad interactions such as views and taps, and diagnostics from the ad SDK
PurposeShowing ads and limiting how often, measuring ads, preventing fraud, and personalized ads if you consent
RecipientGoogle LLC (How Google uses information from partner apps, Google Privacy Policy)

After you buy "Remove Ads", the ad SDK requests no ads. The app privacy details on the App Store and Google Play list only this ad-related data.

③ Payments

"Remove Ads" is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never receive your name, card, payment details or store account; the app only checks whether you own the purchase. Payment processing follows Apple's Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy. Terms of use: on iPhone and iPad, Apple's standard EULA; on Android, the Google Play Terms of Service.

④ What you choose to send

  • Sharing: when you tap "Share" on a memory, a copy of the photo goes to the app you choose (Messages, Photos and so on). You decide where it goes; nothing comes to us.
  • Contacting us: if you email us, we receive your email address and what you write (including any device or app details you add).

3. Children under 14

The app is not directed primarily at children under 14, and we don't directly collect anyone's personal information, including children's. Ads are requested as general-audience ads. If a child under 14 emails us, we use the message only as needed to answer without a legal guardian's consent, and delete it as soon as we notice.

4. How long it's kept

  • On-device information: on your device until you delete it or the app. We keep none of it.
  • Ad-related information: kept by Google under its own policies. We don't receive it.
  • Emails: kept for one year after we've answered, then deleted; deleted at once if you ask.

5. How it's deleted

  • One memory: tap "Delete" on the memory to remove its photo and record (best times stay).
  • A puzzle in progress: long-press it in the puzzle drawer and choose "Remove from Drawer" to delete its photo and progress.
  • Everything: deleting the app removes everything it stored.
  • Device backups: if your device's own backup (iCloud Backup, Google backup) is on, it may also contain the app's data; you can delete backups in your Apple or Google account settings.
  • Emails: when the retention period ends, deleted from our mailbox beyond recovery.

6. Sharing with third parties

We don't give or sell your personal information to anyone. In the free version, Google's ad SDK sends the information in section 2 ② directly to Google; this is not us handing over information we hold, and you can refuse or limit it as described in section 14.

  • Payments: handled directly by Apple or Google (section 2 ③).

7. Further use

Not applicable. We don't use or provide information beyond what relates to the original purposes.

8. Processors

Not applicable. We run no servers and don't outsource processing.

9. Transfers abroad

We send no information abroad ourselves. When the free version requests an ad, information is transferred abroad as follows:

RecipientGoogle LLC (privacy contact: Google privacy form)
CountryThe United States and other countries where Google has data centers
When and howEach time an ad is requested while you use the app, over the network (HTTPS)
DataThe items in section 2 ②
PurposeShowing and measuring ads, preventing fraud, personalized ads if you consent
RetentionUnder Google's policies
How to refuseRefuse personalized ads by declining tracking (ATT), using Privacy Choices in the app, or deleting your Android advertising ID. To stop ad requests altogether, buy "Remove Ads". The puzzle features all keep working.

10. Security measures

  • Puzzle information stays on your device, in the app's private storage protected by the operating system. We have no servers or account system.
  • Photos are read only when you pick them in the system photo picker; the app never asks for whole-library access.
  • Ad and payment traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).

11. Sensitive information

We don't process sensitive information. Photos you pick may show faces or other sensitive content, though. They stay on your device and go only where you send them with "Share", so please check before sharing.

12. Pseudonymized information

Not applicable. We don't process pseudonymized information.

13. Automatic collection and how to refuse it

The app has no cookies or analytics of ours. In the free version, Google's ad SDK uses advertising identifiers and device storage (on iPhone, the IDFA only if you allow it under ATT). How to refuse is in section 14.

This website remembers only your light/dark choice, in your browser's local storage; it isn't sent to us. The site is served by GitHub Pages, so GitHub may log visits, including IP addresses (GitHub Privacy Statement). Web fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your IP address and similar connection details.

14. Behavioral information and ad choices

We don't collect behavioral information ourselves. In the free version, Google may process behavioral information such as advertising identifiers and ad interactions for personalized ads. You can refuse or limit this at any time:

  • iPhone and iPad: choose "Ask App Not to Track" in the prompt shown on first launch, or turn it off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking
  • In the app: where consent applies (the EEA, the UK and similar), change or withdraw it in Settings › Privacy Choices
  • Android: reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Privacy › Ads (the name varies by device)
  • No ads at all: the one-time "Remove Ads" purchase

15. Your rights and how to use them

You (or a legal guardian for children under 14) may ask at any time to access, correct, delete or stop processing your personal information. Since puzzle information lives only on your device, you can do most of this right in the app:

  • Access: see it in the puzzle drawer, the memory album and Settings › Best Times.
  • Correct: edit a memory's note on its detail screen.
  • Delete: delete memories and puzzles one by one, or delete the app to remove everything (section 5).
  • Stop processing or withdraw consent: stop ad-related processing as described in section 14. The puzzle features keep working.
  • For ad-related data held by Google, use Google's privacy tools (Google Account › Data & privacy).
  • The only information we hold is email you send us. Ask the privacy officer below to see or delete it and we'll act promptly (within 10 days at the latest).

If you live outside Korea, you can use the rights your local law gives you (for example the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California) in the same way. We don't sell or share personal information (including for cross-context behavioral advertising) and won't treat you differently for using your rights.

16. Automated decisions

Not applicable. We make no fully automated decisions that significantly affect your rights or obligations.
