Memory Pieces · for iPhone, iPad and Android

Every photo
is a puzzle of memories

Travel snaps, family moments, your dog on the couch. Pick a photo you love, cut it into 9 to 64 jigsaw pieces and put the day back together, piece by piece. When the last piece clicks in, the seams melt away and your photo is whole again.

🧩 9 · 16 · 36 · 64 pieces📷 Your own photos💡 Hints and guide🗂️ Puzzle drawer📒 Memory album⏱️ Best times🎵 Gentle music📴 Works offline

How it works

  1. Pick a photo from your library. The app only sees the photo you pick.
  2. Frame the part you want and choose 9, 16, 36 or 64 pieces.
  3. Drag pieces from the tray onto the board. Close to the right spot, they click into place.
  4. When the last piece snaps in, the seams fade and your photo is whole again. Add a note and keep it in your memory album.

Small, but carefully made

🧩 Real jigsaw shapes

Every piece has its own tabs and blanks and a slightly raised, cardboard-like look. Pinch to zoom, or show edge pieces only.

💡 Hints when you're stuck

Three free hints per puzzle, plus a faint grayscale guide image (games with hints or the guide don't count toward best times).

🗂️ Puzzle drawer

Keep up to 3 puzzles going. Close the app and pick up exactly where you left off.

📒 Memory album

Finished puzzles are kept with the date, your time and a short note. Share them, or play again at another level.

Your photos stay on your device

No account, no server. The photos you pick, your puzzles and memories stay on your device and never come to us. Ads in the free version are shown by Google AdMob, and you can turn off personalized ads any time. See the privacy policy.
