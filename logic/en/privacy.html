Mochi Noki Logic Privacy Policy

1. Why we process information

We process information only for the purposes below. If a purpose changes, we will take the steps required by law.

  • Providing command card puzzles and sorting races, and saving stars and progress
  • Counting hint use to apply the limit of 2 a day
  • Answering you when you contact us directly, for example by email

We never use information for anything else. We don't sell it, build profiles or combine it with other information.

2. What information is processed

① Information handled only on your device

The game runs entirely on your device. The information below is saved only in the app's own storage, is never sent to us, and is erased when you delete the app. It can't identify who you are.

  • Game progress: stars for each board, opened boards and worlds
  • Customizing and settings: Kongkong's wing color, sound effects, music and other settings
  • Hint use: hints used today and the date (UTC, for the limit of 2 a day)

The app doesn't ask for or collect names, birthdays, school grades, contacts, photos, location or advertising identifiers. It doesn't request camera, microphone, photo, contacts or location permission.

② Information sent off the device

None. The app has no ad SDKs, no analytics and no servers of ours.

③ Payments

The app is free, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Downloads are handled by Apple's App Store under the Apple Privacy Policy, and the terms of use are the Apple Standard EULA.

④ Information you send us

  • Inquiries: if you email us, we receive your email address and your message, including any device or app details you include.

3. Children's information

The app is made for children, including elementary school students. We don't collect anyone's personal information in the app, children included, so no processing in the app requires a parent's consent. The app has no ads, no in-app purchases, no prompts to buy through outside links and no chat with other users.

If a child under 14 contacts us by email, we use the information only as needed to answer and, without a parent's consent, delete it as soon as we notice.

4. How long information is kept

  • On-device information: stays on your device until you delete the app. We don't keep it.
  • Email inquiries: kept for 1 year after we handle them, then deleted. Deleted right away if you ask.

5. How information is deleted

  • Everything: deleting the app erases everything it saved.
  • Device backups: if device backup (iCloud Backup) is on, app data may be in that backup. You can delete backups in your Apple Account settings.
  • Email inquiries: when the retention period ends, deleted so they can't be recovered.

6. Sharing with third parties

We don't provide or sell your personal information to third parties.

7. Additional use or provision

Not applicable. We don't use or provide information beyond what relates to the original purpose.

8. Outsourcing

Not applicable. We run no servers and outsource no processing.

9. Transfers abroad

Not applicable. The app sends no information to us or to third parties.

10. How information is protected

  • Game information stays only in the app's own storage on your device, protected by the operating system. We have no servers or account system.
  • The app doesn't request device permissions such as camera, photos or location.

11. Sensitive information

Not applicable. We don't process sensitive information, and the app has no way to post text or photos.

12. Pseudonymous information

Not applicable. We don't process pseudonymous information.

13. Automatic collection and how to refuse it

The app has no cookies, advertising identifiers or analytics.

This website remembers only your choice of light or dark mode, in your browser's local storage, and never sends it to us. The site is served by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log access such as IP addresses (GitHub Privacy Statement). Web fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google may receive access information such as your IP address.

14. Behavioral information

Not applicable. The app has no ads, and neither we nor third parties collect behavioral information for targeted advertising.

15. Your rights and how to use them

You (or a parent or guardian for children under 14) can ask at any time to see, correct, delete or stop the processing of personal information. Game information is only on your device, so you can do this in the app.

  • See: stars and progress are shown on the world and board screens.
  • Delete: deleting the app erases everything (section 5).
  • The only information we hold is email you send us. Ask the privacy officer below to see or delete it and we will do so without delay, within 10 days at the latest.

Users outside Korea can use their rights under local law (such as the EU and UK GDPR, or COPPA and CCPA/CPRA in the US) the same way. We don't sell or share personal information and never treat you differently for using your rights.

16. Automated decisions

Not applicable. We make no fully automated decisions that significantly affect your rights or obligations.

17. Privacy officer

For questions, complaints or remedies about personal information, contact us below. We will answer and act without delay.

  • Privacy officer: the operator of Mochi Noki Logic
  • Email: hello.mochinoki@gmail.com

18. Domestic representative

Not applicable. The operator is located in Korea.

20. Video surveillance

Not applicable. We operate no fixed or mobile video devices.

21. Changes to this policy

This policy applies from November 18, 2026. If online features such as rankings or friends change what information is processed, we will announce it before it takes effect on this page, on What's new and in the app.
