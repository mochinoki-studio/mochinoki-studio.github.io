Support

Mochi Noki Logic FAQ and how to contact us

How do I start?
Pick a world on the home screen and tap an open board. Tapping a card below adds it to the command row in order; tapping a card in the command row removes it. When you're done, press Run.
How do repeat cards work?
When you place a repeat card, new cards go inside it. Tap the number on the repeat card to change the count from 2 to 9, and tap "+ add" again to go back to adding cards at the end.
How do I earn stars?
In command puzzles, stars depend on how many cards you used. At or under the fewest possible, you get three stars; two more cards gets two. In sorting races, you get one star for finishing, one for beating Mallangi and one for using the fewest swaps.
Is everything really free?
Yes. All 10 worlds, daily puzzles, practice puzzles, learning reports and wing colors are free. There are no ads or in-app purchases.
How many hints can I use?
Up to 2 a day. They refill to 2 at the same moment worldwide, 00:00 UTC (9 AM in Korea and Japan). If you're out of hints, place just a few cards and run them to see how far Kongkong gets.
It's too hard
Open the problem description at the top of the board again. Instead of building everything at once, place a few cards and run them to see how far Kongkong gets.
What age is it for?
A first grader who can read a little can play alone. Worlds follow the math curriculum, not age, so adults can enjoy it from the start too.
Does my progress move to a new device?
Not yet. There is no server, so stars and progress are stored only on the device.
How do I delete my records?
Deleting the app erases all records. See section 5 of the Privacy Policy.

Contact

Send questions and bug reports to the addresses below. For bug reports, these details help us check quickly:

  • Device model and OS version (for example, iPhone 15, iOS 26.1)
  • App version (Settings › Version)
  • Which board, what you were doing, what happened, and whether it happens again
