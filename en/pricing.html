Pricing

Mochi Noki is free, with no ads and no account. Noki Plus is an option for those who want more.

Free

  • Five friends (Mongsil, Boksle, Tangtang, Kongkong, Mallo), growing, awakening and graduating
  • Water, stretching and the focus timer
  • One habit of your own plus the built-in ones (focus, stretching, water), plans and Mac Calendar
  • Quests, achievements, challenges and snacks
  • 16 outfits bought with snacks, plus outfits earned with achievements and awakening
  • Three mini games and battles with friends

Noki Plus

  • Unlimited habits
  • Noki's monthly letter and report
  • Seasonal friends (Eunhaeng and Danpung in autumn, Noonsongi and Hoppang in winter)
  • Four Plus outfits (satin bow, rose-gold glasses, lace collar, fairy wings)
  • 1.5× snacks
  • Seasonal friends you meet and outfits you take stay after Plus ends

Noki Plus plans

Monthly$2.99
Yearly$19.99
Lifetime (one-time purchase)$39.99

Prices in US dollars. Apple sets the price in each region, and the app's shop shows yours.

Noki Plus purchases open once the app launches on the App Store. Until then, every free feature is yours.

Subscription terms

Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically for the same period and price unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date, and payment is charged to your Apple Account. Manage or cancel in your App Store account settings ("Manage subscription" in the shop). On another Mac or after reinstalling, use Shop › Plus › "Restore purchases".
