Mochi Noki Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026 · Version 1.3

This policy explains how the operator of Mochi Noki ("we") handles information in the Mochi Noki app for macOS and on this website. It follows the structure required by Korea's Personal Information Protection Act (Article 30).

This English version is provided for reference. The Korean version is authoritative; if the two differ, the Korean version prevails.

At a glance
🙅 Nothing collectedWe don't collect your personal information or keep it on any server. No accounts, ads or analytics. 💻 On your MacYour pet and records are processed and stored only on your Mac. 📴 Nothing sent to usThe app sends no information to us or anyone else. If you turn on iCloud sync, your pet and records are kept in your own iCloud, where we can't see them.

1. Why information is processed

Information is processed only for these purposes; if they change, we'll update this policy first.

  • Raising your pet and keeping your daily records (water, stretching, focus, habits, plans)
  • If you turn it on: showing and reminding you of Mac Calendar events
  • If you turn it on: syncing your pet and records between your Macs through your own iCloud
  • Checking whether you have Noki Plus
  • Answering you when you contact us (for example by email)

We use information only for these purposes. We never use it for advertising, tracking, selling or building profiles, and never combine it with other information.

2. What is processed

① Processed on your Mac (on-device)

Mochi Noki's features process information on your device. It isn't sent to any server, and it's deleted from your Mac when you delete a record or turn the feature off. macOS keeps an app's data folder even after the app is moved to the Trash; how to remove everything: section 5.

  • Your pet: name, stage and look, stats, outfits, album, diary, collection, achievements and quest progress
  • What you enter: habits and their history, plans (title, date, time, note), water logs and cups, mini game records
  • Settings: reminders, appearance, language, notch side and so on
  • Mac status (not stored; only feeds the pet's daily counts): battery level and charging, thermal state, CPU load, time since the last keyboard or mouse input (never what you type), whether the screen is asleep, and whether the front window fills the screen (app names, window titles and contents are never read)
  • Mac Calendar (only if you connect it and allow access in macOS): the next seven days of events in the calendars you pick, read to show and remind you, never stored. If you turn on "Add my plans to Calendar", plans made in the app are written to the calendar you choose, and the app only changes or deletes the events it added. If you change an event the app added in the Calendar app, its title, date, time and note are updated in the app's plan too.
  • Backups: "Export backup" writes the above to a file where you choose. On updates the app keeps copies of the previous save (the last three), a copy made just before you import a backup, and a copy of any save a new version couldn't read (kept aside rather than deleted), all in the same folder.

The app uses no camera, microphone, contacts, macOS Location Services (GPS or Wi-Fi location), Accessibility, Input Monitoring or Screen Recording access. It never reads what you type, other apps' names, window titles or documents, or the websites you visit.

② Sent out

Nothing is sent to us. The app has no network permission and we run no servers. Only if you turn on ⚙️ › "Sync with iCloud" (or "Raise Noki with your other Macs (iCloud)" in the welcome), your whole pet and records (one signed save file, a few dozen KB), a random identifier for that Mac and the time it was last used are stored in iCloud (the CloudKit private database) under your own Apple Account. We can't access this data; Apple keeps it under the Apple Privacy Policy. Turn it off and syncing stops. The weather feature of earlier versions is gone in this version; a city typed before stays inside the app, unused. The App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

③ Payments

Noki Plus purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple's App Store. We never receive payment or Apple Account details; the app only checks the signed purchase records Apple keeps on your Mac to see whether Plus is on. See the Apple Privacy Policy. A subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date; manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings (in the app: Shop › Plus › "Manage subscription"). The Terms of Use are Apple's standard license agreement (EULA).

④ What you send yourself

  • Battle codes: "Copy my code" puts your pet's name, stage and look, level, stats, tricks, outfits, pattern and random pet and collection identifiers on your clipboard. It reaches only whoever you paste it to, never us. A friend's code is read from the clipboard only when you choose "Battle the code I copied", and the app keeps only the friend pet's name and look needed for the battle.
  • Feedback and questions: "Send feedback" only copies the app and macOS versions, Mac model, display setup, language and a short pet summary to your clipboard; you decide whether to send it. If you email us, we receive your email address and message.

3. Children under 14

There is no sign-up, and we collect no one's personal information, children included. If a child under 14 emails us, we use the message only as needed to answer and delete it once we learn of it, unless a parent or guardian consents.

4. How long it's kept

  • On your Mac: until you delete it. We keep nothing.
  • Copies of the previous save made on updates: the last three; older ones are deleted automatically.
  • Data in iCloud (if you turn sync on): until you delete it from your iCloud. Before switching to the pet in iCloud, the app keeps up to ten copies of this Mac's save on the Mac.
  • Emails: kept for one year after we answer, then deleted, or right away if you ask.

5. How it's deleted

  • App data: move the app to the Trash and, in Finder, delete the "Mochi Noki" folder under ~/Library/Containers/. That removes your pet and records.
  • Per feature: disconnecting Calendar clears the events read; events the app added to Calendar can be deleted in the Calendar app.
  • Exported backups: delete them where you saved them.
  • iCloud data: in System Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage storage, delete the Mochi Noki data.
  • Emails: deleted beyond recovery when the retention period ends.

6. Sharing with third parties

We don't share or sell personal information to third parties. The app contains no third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising or crash reporting.

  • Payments: Apple processes App Store payments directly, under the Apple Privacy Policy (section 2 ③).
  • iCloud sync: you keep the data in your own Apple Account; we don't share it with anyone (section 2 ②).

7. Further use

Not applicable. We don't use or share information beyond the purposes above.

8. Processors

Not applicable. We run no servers and use no processors.

9. Transfers abroad

We send no information abroad. If you turn on iCloud sync, your data may be stored on Apple's servers, including outside your country. Recipient: Apple Inc. · Data: as in section 2 ② · When and how: to iCloud whenever the save changes while sync is on · Purpose: syncing between your Macs · Kept: until you delete it · To refuse: turn sync off (everything else keeps working).

10. Security measures

  • Information is processed only on your Mac; we have no servers or account systems.
  • The app runs in the macOS App Sandbox and reaches only what you allow.
  • The app has no network permission. The only information that leaves your Mac is iCloud sync if you turn it on, stored only in Apple's encrypted iCloud under your own account.

11. Sensitive information

Not applicable. We don't process sensitive information.

12. Pseudonymized information

Not applicable.

13. Cookies and similar technologies

The app and this website use no cookies, analytics or advertising identifiers. The website remembers only your light or dark choice in your browser's local storage, which is never sent to us. This website is served by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visits, including IP addresses (GitHub Privacy Statement). Its fonts load from Google Fonts. Google may receive your IP address and similar connection details when the fonts load (Google Privacy Policy). The app opens website links in your browser and never loads web content inside the app.

14. Behavioral information and ads

Not applicable. We collect no behavioral information for advertising, and the app has no ads.

15. Your rights

You (or a parent or guardian for children under 14) can ask to see, correct, delete or stop the processing of your information at any time. Since it lives on your Mac, you can do most of this yourself in the app.

  • See: in the app, or all of it as a file with ⚙️ › Data › Export backup
  • Correct: edit your pet's name, habits, plans and water logs in the app
  • Delete: per record, or everything as in section 5
  • Stop or withdraw consent: turn Mac Calendar (⚙️ › Mac Calendar) off at any time and reading stops right away. You can also withdraw access in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars. Everything else keeps working.
  • iCloud sync: turn off ⚙️ › "Sync with iCloud" and it stops right away. Delete data already in iCloud as in section 5.
  • The only information we hold is email you send us. Ask the privacy officer below to see or delete it and we'll act without delay (within 10 days at the latest).

16. Automated decisions

Not applicable. We make no fully automated decisions that significantly affect you. If such a feature is ever added, this policy will be updated first.
